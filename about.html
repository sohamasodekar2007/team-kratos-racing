About Us

Team Kratos Racing

The Official Formula Student Team of PCCOE. Engineering excellence, racing passion, and global competitive spirit.

Rank 7 Global | Only Indian Team in Top 10

Who We Are

43 Enthusiastic Engineering Undergraduates

Team Kratos Racing is a nonprofit student organization working in the Incubation Center of PCCOE. We are dedicated to designing, manufacturing, and racing high-performance formula-style cars.

We are proud to be the only Indian team in the Top 10 World Rankings (2023), securing Rank 7 globally and setting new standards for engineering excellence in India.
